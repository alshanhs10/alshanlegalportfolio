About

Building a legal legacy with integrity, intellect & impact.

Alshan Husain Shah is a Law Graduate and Gold Medalist in English Honours, with Hands-on Experience in High Court Litigation, Drafting, Legal Research and Analytical Legal Writing.

The present role involves assisting with pleadings, legal research, case briefs and preparation for hearings across diverse matters before the Hon'ble High Court of Lucknow. Prior court-based experience has further provided exposure to litigation support, legal notices, drafting, arbitration, contracts, compliance research and case analysis, complemented by internships with legal professionals and organizations.

Alongside practical legal work, Alshan has developed a strong interest in legal writing and research. Published work covers subjects including Criminal Law, LegalTech, Contracts and Artificial Intelligence, reflecting an ongoing interest in both substantive legal issues and the changing methods through which legal professionals are evolving with technology.

A background in English Honours, culminating in Two University Gold Medals as Departmental and Faculty Topper, has contributed to a strong command of language, analytical reasoning and structured communication. Combined with legal education and courtroom exposure, this foundation supports an approach to legal work grounded in precision, clarity, research and continuous learning.
